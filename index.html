// PROTOCOL_LORE: ORIGINS

Neo-Atacama didn’t sleep; it just pulsed with erratic voltage under a polluted sky. Built over the bones of an ancient submerged basin, the city was a labyrinth of glowing holographic ads, rust-caked server banks, and wet, reflected neon.

In the sub-levels, Kael "Zero" stared at a flickering monitor inside a makeshift rig built from scavenged chips. He wasn't a hero, just a low-tier technician trying to pay off his grid tax. But everything changed the moment he tapped into a deep corporate pipeline and stumbled upon an anomaly—a fragment of corrupted code that didn't belong to any known protocol. It was a Glitch Pixel, a sentient piece of digital consciousness.

Together with Vesper V and Spark-E, the three outcasts slipped into the dark networks of Neo-Atacama. The System Crash had begun.
