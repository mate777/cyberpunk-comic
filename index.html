// PROTOCOL_LORE: ORIGINS

Neo-Atacama didn’t sleep; it just pulsed with erratic voltage under a polluted sky. Built over the bones of an ancient submerged basin, the city was a labyrinth of glowing holographic ads, rust-caked server banks, and wet, reflected neon.

In the sub-levels, Kael "Zero" stared at a flickering monitor inside a makeshift rig built from scavenged chips. He wasn't a hero, just a low-tier technician trying to pay off his grid tax. But everything changed the moment he tapped into a deep corporate pipeline and stumbled upon an anomaly—a fragment of corrupted code that didn't belong to any known protocol. It was a Glitch Pixel, a sentient piece of digital consciousness. The moment Kael touched the sequence, it seared itself into his neural interface, leaving him with an uncanny ability: he could erase his digital footprint in real time, fading like a phantom from any scanner.

He didn't stay hidden for long. A burst of red laser-sights sliced through the dense smog of his workshop. Vesper V had arrived. An elite mercenary with prototype Overclock implants visible beneath her high-collar tactical vest, Vesper was legendary in the undercity. Sent by the corporate Syndicate to retrieve the anomaly at any cost, she pinned Kael against the rusted wall. But as her visual HUD scanned his eye, it didn't register a target—it displayed the raw code of the System Glitch. Vesper froze. The data matched the encrypted breach that had compromised her own augmentations months ago. Realizing the Syndicate had betrayed her, she lowered her weapon. "You're coming with me," she rasped. "Before the sweepers burn this whole block."

As they broke through the alleyways, a spherical metallic drone swooped down from the cables above. Spark-E, an autonomous AI bot carrying forgotten archives of the old web, projected a wireframe map ahead of them. Spark-E had been tracking the glitch's frequency, preserving fragments of a forgotten history before the city was flooded. Together, the three outcasts slipped into the dark networks of Neo-Atacama. The System Crash had begun.
